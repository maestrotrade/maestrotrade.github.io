Install and compile the EA

Open the desktop MT4 terminal for your Forex broker and extract the WIN_001 ZIP.

1

Open the MT4 data folder

In the terminal you intend to use, select File → Open Data Folder. Open the MQL4 folder.

2

Copy the source files

From the ZIP’s NEW Data folder, put MAESTRO_WRAPPER_EA.mq4 directly in MQL4/Experts. Copy the five include files directly into MQL4/Include.

MAESTRO_ENGINE.mqh
MAESTRO_ENTRY.mqh
MAESTRO_LEARNING.mqh
MAESTRO_RESEARCH.mqh
MAESTRO_SAFETY.mqh
3

Build the Expert Advisor

Press F4 in MT4 to open MetaEditor. Open MAESTRO_WRAPPER_EA.mq4 and press F7 to compile. Check for zero errors. A successful compile creates MAESTRO_WRAPPER_EA.ex4 in MQL4/Experts.

Attach the EA to an M1 Forex chart

With AutoTrading off, open your broker’s chart for the Forex pair and select M1. In Navigator → Expert Advisors, refresh the list and drag MAESTRO_WRAPPER_EA onto the chart. Use one EA instance per pair you intend to trade. The engine evaluates the symbol of its host chart.

Configure the EA inputs

Open the EA properties and select Inputs.

InputWhat it controls
AssetSelectorKeep the default value of 1 for Forex.
EnableTradingSet to true when ready to test order placement on demo.
RiskCurrencyChoose CAD (default), USD, EUR or GBP. If the selected currency differs from the account deposit currency, the EA uses available broker quotes to convert the limits.
LossWallCAD and CloseTriggerCADThe default loss objective is 0.25 and the early close trigger is 0.19. With USD selected, those values mean US$0.25 and US$0.19. The inputs retain their original CAD suffixes.
DailyLossCAD and technical risk inputsConfigure the daily loss threshold and the initial and combined stop-based risk limits. These monetary settings use the selected risk currency. Margin thresholds are percentages.
M1, M5 and combination indicator inputsAdjust the periods and thresholds exposed for supported strategies, including EMA, RSI, ADX, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic, ATR, CCI and Donchian settings. Available settings vary by strategy.

What runs underneath the wrapper

Tick and candle signals

The engine evaluates 1-second and 5-second tick windows, closed M1 setups, and closed M5 setups and indicator combinations. The internal profiles labeled M15 and M30 each evaluate five variants using closed M5 candles.

Shared trade management

Candidate signals pass through shared confirmation and risk checks before an order can be sent. An open trade for the same symbol and EA magic number blocks new entries for that instance.

Activate and verify on demo

1

Check initialization

In the MT4 Experts tab, look for Win_001 INIT. Verify the chart symbol, risk currency, account currency and displayed limits.

2

Enable trading

On the demo account, enable Allow live trading in the EA’s Common tab and turn on the terminal’s AutoTrading button. Verify that EnableTrading is true in the EA inputs.

3

Review activity

Keep MT4 running and connected. Use Experts and Journal to review initialization, order attempts and broker messages. Check the Trade and Account History tabs for open and closed orders.

Review trade and signal logs

Use the EA’s signal, trade and research logs to review candidate confirmation, rejected orders and trade outcomes. Several WIN_001 CSV telemetry files use MetaTrader’s shared Common/Files folder. Keep your demo logs when comparing changes to indicator or risk inputs.

Troubleshooting

The EA does not appear in Navigator

Confirm you compiled the wrapper in the data folder for this MT4 terminal. Check that the five include files are directly in MQL4/Include, then refresh Expert Advisors.

Initialization reports that risk FX is unavailable

Check the selected risk currency and the account’s deposit currency. Confirm your broker provides conversion quotes between them, or select the deposit currency if it is one of the four supported choices.

The EA loads but I see no orders

Check the M1 chart, trading permissions, market hours, available M1 and M5 history, and the Experts and Journal messages. A setup must qualify, pass risk checks and receive broker acceptance before it can open a trade.
